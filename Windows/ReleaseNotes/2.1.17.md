# CodeRim for Windows 2.1.17

Interface polish for the Windows app. x64 and ARM64 MSI installers; macOS is unchanged at 2.1.15.

- The settings and account icons sit exactly in the round ends of the notch's control rail. They were centred on the icon font's line box, which sits low and off-centre.
- The controls move like macOS: the resting arc shrinks away as the gear grows in and turns upright with a spring, and the rail lengthens from the gear toward the account control.
- Provider rings in the notch only show their usage card on hover, as on macOS; clicking a ring no longer refreshes it or opens a session.
- The Usage provider menu lists every added provider. Claude Code appears before it is connected and explains how to connect; other providers open their own usage page.
- The settings window has Windows 11's rounded corners again, and its cards, buttons, fields and menus use rounder corners closer to the macOS settings.

Existing 2.1.x installations update themselves; the MSI and its SHA-256 files are attached as before.
