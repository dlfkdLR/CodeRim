# CodeRim 2.1.26

A macOS-only release that makes the time-left estimate honest about big tasks. Windows stays at 2.1.20.

## Time left

- **Tool calls.** A turn that has called many tools is a build, whatever its age, so it is compared only with past turns that went at least that far. On this Mac's own history, a Claude turn at 100 seconds with 25 calls reads about 6 to 12 minutes typical where one with none reads about 1.5 to 6. Claude's calls are counted from its transcript, reading on from where the last look stopped; past turns are filed with their call counts when the logs are read at launch, and history kept by older versions gains them then. Codex and OpenCode do not report this yet and are sized by time as before. A turn first seen halfway through counts only the calls made since.
- **A quiet start.** The first 90 seconds of a turn carry no estimate: most turns end inside them, and the ones that do not are the ones a typical-turn estimate is most wrong about. The card keeps its ring, turning, with "Working" in the middle, then the ring fills as before.
- **A fit for every range.** A range whose ends are in different units used to be cut off inside the ring ("25 sec – 3…"). It now reads "25s–3m" and "1h10m–2h".

## Verification

macOS: full local test suite with 0 failures, run with warnings as errors as CI does. Importing 60 days of Claude transcripts, now with call counts, took about 4 seconds on the development Mac. The card was checked by hand in the running app.
