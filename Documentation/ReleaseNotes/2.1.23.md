# CodeRim 2.1.23

A macOS-only release: it redraws the chat details card and lets finished Claude chats leave the notch's list. Windows stays at 2.1.20.

## Chat details card

- The small icon tiles are replaced by a ring and a column. The ring fills as the turn goes and holds the typical range ("16–29 min left"); a paused turn holds its place and compaction makes it breathe. Chats with no estimate show the column alone.
- The column shows the model in the card-title type, its reasoning effort, then elapsed time, tokens, finish time and the longest the turn is expected to take, two to a line, and the Fast mode and Daybreak switches as filled or hollow dots. Labels are grey body type and values white, matching the usage card beside it.

## Claude chats

- A Claude chat that has sat idle for 90 seconds leaves the notch's list, the same as a Codex chat. Chats that are working, waiting for you or compacting stay.

## Verification

macOS: full local test suite with 0 failures. The card was checked by hand in the running app on the development Mac.
